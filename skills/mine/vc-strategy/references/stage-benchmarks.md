# Stage Benchmarks — the numbers, with vintages and conflicts

Historical benchmarks retain their stated vintage. Verify current primary sources before using a number for present financial guidance; no benchmark is a permanent eligibility rule.

Single source of truth for current-market bars (vc-pitch-deck points here). Every figure carries source + year; **treat anything pre-2024 as historical**. Conflicts are stated, not averaged — quote the population behind a number, or don't quote it.

## Pre-seed (2025–26)

- Round: mean check $1.52M (FundraiseInsider, early 2025); ~42% of Carta pre-seed rounds under $250K (Q3 2024); the $1–2.5M middle band is shrinking — 24% (Q1 2023) → 18% (Q1 2026, Carta).
- Instruments: SAFEs ~90% of rounds; convertible notes at a record-low 7% (Carta, Q1 2026). SAFE caps from ~$7.5M for rounds under $2M, scaling with size (Carta via CRV).
- AI's share of pre-seed dollars: ~50%, up from ~30% a few years prior (Carta, Q1 2026).
- Traction bar: ~89% of funded pre-seeds had *something* (LOIs, pipeline, beta, testimonials) — only ~11% pure idea stage (DocSend, 2021).
- Runway target 12–18 months; graduation pre-seed→seed ≈ 50% (Incisive, 2025).

## Seed (2025–26)

- Round size: $2.5–5M; hot AI/deep-tech $6–10M (startups.com/Carta, 2025). Median deal $3.1M (PitchBook/NVCA, 2024).
- Valuation — three populations, don't mix: median $12.8M (Carta 2024, all-seed broad cut) · $20–30M post, median ~$24M (startups.com 2025, priced-seed cut) · AI seed median pre-money $17.9M, ~42% above non-AI peers (eqvista, 2026).
- Dilution: 18–25% typical; actual 20.1% (Carta 2024); 25–30% with stacked SAFEs converting. Founder floor: ≥70% collective post-seed; pool 12–15% post-money.
- Traction bar (2026): $500K–$1.5M ARR for a *strong* B2B SaaS seed (CRV) — revenue is now the baseline; qualitative-pull seeds are the exception path (deep tech, infra, unusual models), not the default. Alternate framings: $5K–$50K MRR (startups.com), 50–500 users consumer, 5–15 enterprise pilots.
- Growth read: 15–20%+ MoM strong, >25% exceptional (CRV); YC's "10%/week sustained is interesting."
- Quality bars: monthly churn <2.5–5%; NRR ≥100% (great ≥105%); CAC payback <12 months (best-in-class 5–7); LTV:CAC ~3:1 at maturity (CRV, 2026).
- Process: 3–6 months end-to-end; 40–60+ meetings to land ≤15 investors; a raise dragging past ~4 months is itself a negative signal (CRV).

## Seed → Series A reality (the number that should drive sizing)

- Conversion: **30–35%**, down from ~50% pre-2022 (startups.com/Carta, 2025). UK: only 12% of seeded startups ever reach A (Antler).
- Timing: median ~20 months; **39% now take 3+ years** (vs 19% in Q3 2019); only 23% within 1.5–2 years; 15% within 12 months (Carta, 3,365 startups, via Lemkin Oct 2025).
- Consequence: plan the seed for ~3 years — burn $75–100K/month, not $200K ("Raise more. Burn less. Plan for 3 years." — Lemkin).
- The 20-month bar walk (startups.com 2025 — what the seed must buy, same SaaS company): ARR $200K → $1.5–2M (7.5–10×) · net-new ARR $20K/mo → $80–120K/mo · team 4 → 12–18 · cash 75–85% burned · NDR 105–120% · gross margin 70–80% · logo retention >85% · burn multiple 1.5–2.5× (>3× yellow flag).

## Series A (2025–26)

- ARR bar is a **range, argued not asserted**: $1M–$2M clears the door (startups.com 2025) · $1.5M–$3M (SaaS CFO, Oct 2025) · **$2M–$5M wins a competitive process** (CRV 2026) · "$3M is the new $1M" (Graham & Walker 2025). Reality check: 35.6% of A rounds had zero revenue, 69.8% under $1M ARR (Carta 2024, actuals) — the advice bar is far above the actual distribution; the gap is survivorship of *competitive* processes.
- Growth: 2–3× YoY top-tier; the bar on the way to $10M ARR moved 3× → 5×+ (Lemkin, Oct 2025); "the average VC-backed company at ~$1M run-rate quadrupled YoY" (SignalFire, 2021 — dated).
- Round: $5M–$15M (Carta via CRV); median valuation $41.5M (Carta 2024); dilution median 17.9% (Carta Q1 2025); AI Series A: $8–15M at $30–50M pre (eqvista, 2026); AI valuation premium +38% (Carta, 2025).
- Quality: gross margin 70%+ (80%+ increasingly expected); NRR 110–120%+ shows pricing power; GRR is the health metric ("low GRR is a big handicap on your valuation"); magic number >1.0; LTV:CAC ~3–4×; logo retention >85% annual or diligence fails; ARR ÷ ACV ≥80% for enterprise.
- Concentration: top-10 funds took ~43% of all VC dollars (PitchBook/NVCA, Q3 2025); Series A deal count −18% YoY (Carta, Q4 2024).

## Bridge rounds (Carta 2024 medians — the only bridge data in the corpus)

- Seed→A bridge ("seed+"): $20M valuation vs $12.8M seed median. A→B bridge: $52.1M vs $41.5M. B→C bridge: $105.8M — *below* the $118.4M Series B median.
- Pricing rules: existing investors 10–25% discount to next round; new money pre-round asks ~2× step-up (Lemkin).

## Fund-side numbers (for computing any fund's math)

- Check ≈ 2% of fund size for 10–20% ownership (Lemkin). Seed leads target 10–20%; Series A leads ~20–25%.
- Seed funds see ~1,000 companies/year, invest in 4–5; A partners: 1–2 deals/partner/year (NFX).
- Funnel: ~1 in 4 opportunities → meeting; ⅓ of those → partner meeting; ~½ of PMs → diligence; ~⅓ of diligence → term sheet ⇒ 1–2% overall (INSEAD via Qubit, 2026). Post-first-1:1 odds ≈5%; post-PM offer rate 25–60% at quoted firms (First Round, 2024).

## Environment (Q1 2026 snapshot — decays fastest)

- $300B across ~6,000 startups in Q1 2026, +150% QoQ/YoY; one quarter ≈ 70% of all 2025 deployment (Crunchbase via Qubit). Mega-rounds: OpenAI $122B; Anthropic $30B; xAI $20B.
- AI ≈ 50% of global VC (2025, from 34% in 2024). Devtools comp set founders get measured against: Cursor ~$3B ARR; Cognition $1B at $26B with Devin at $492M ARR (20VC, Jun 2026).
- Median seed close: 142 days (2025) vs 68 (2021) — slower is normal now.
