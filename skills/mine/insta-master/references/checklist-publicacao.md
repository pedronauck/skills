# Instagram Publication Review

Review the requested artifact against its actual audience, intent, evidence, and current platform requirements. Use only applicable concerns; a caption edit does not require a channel strategy, account audit, or post-publication experiment.

- The content makes a clear, accurate promise and delivers it. Reuse the established audience and objective instead of repeating an interview.
- The opening, caption, cover, text, audio, and pacing support the intended format. Keep information accessible, including useful captions/alt text where supported.
- A CTA should help the intended next action when one is needed. Curiosity gaps, plot twists, a specific caption formula, or exactly one CTA are not universal requirements.
- Follow current format limits and diagnose actual readability, retention, or discovery issues. Corpus timing, hashtag, topic, posting-frequency, and retention figures are hypotheses, not publication thresholds.
- Respect content/image/audio rights, consent, and applicable disclosure rules. Do not invent engagement, buy followers, or turn automation into spam. Check account restrictions when an observed failure points there; an account audit is not required for every draft.
- Helper scores are advisory. Fix a relevant finding rather than rewriting valid content to satisfy a lexical or numeric heuristic.

Publishing, sending replies, or starting an ongoing monitoring campaign requires the user's authorization. When a post-publication analysis was requested, use the account's actual data and a suitable measurement window. Do not hold a finished draft open for an automatic first-hour watch or a 30-day follow-up.
