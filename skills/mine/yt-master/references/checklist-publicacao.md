# Publication review

Apply only to the video and format being prepared for publication. Existing decisions need no new approval round; drafting or editing a title does not require completing this whole review.

- The title and thumbnail make an honest, specific promise that the video delivers. Text is readable at its intended display size; assets use the correct dimensions for the format.
- The opening orients the intended viewer. The script and editing support the actual payoff, with no mandatory hook duration, named technique, title quota, or artificial suspense.
- Descriptions, captions, chapters, links, and accessibility features are accurate where applicable. Verify actual processing/review status rather than treating a fixed waiting period as readiness evidence.
- Third-party footage, audio, sponsorship, or monetization use follows the applicable rights and current platform rules. Verify changing limits when relevant; do not copy dated corpus numbers as policy.
- Any measurement plan compares appropriate audience, traffic source, format, and window. A CTR difference alone does not require a thumbnail swap; a meaningful experiment depends on sample and context.
- Publish only within the user's authorization. No extra alternatives, channel calendar, or mandatory post-publication experiment is needed to finish an already-supported artifact.
